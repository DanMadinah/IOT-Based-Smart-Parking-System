What it does
  • Opens the gate only for a car that has paid and confirmed it is at the gate.
  • Lets a car out only when its owner taps I'm exiting on that car's ticket.
  • Counts cars in and out with two IR sensors, and refuses entry when the lot is full.
  • Raises security alerts for unpaid cars, unconfirmed arrivals, repeated wrong codes, refused exits and tailgating.
  • Warns with a buzzer when something is too close to the wall.
  • Shows everything live on this website and on the LCD.
Limits
  • The website opens only on the ESP32's Wi-Fi.
  • Payment is a demo and uses no real money.
  • The system checks payment, presence and tickets, not which car is at the gate. A number-plate camera is future work.
